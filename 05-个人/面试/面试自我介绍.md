## 📝 正文内容

**适用场景**：正式面试开场，面试官说"请简单介绍一下你自己"。

### 【开场】

面试官您好，我叫刘方轩。拥有4年的Python开发经验。我的技术栈主要集中在后端开发与自动化测试领域，同时也具备一定的前端和数据处理能力，能够独立负责从需求分析到部署上线的全流程开发。

### 【工作经历与核心技能】

在过去的工作中，我主要积累了两方面的核心经验：

第一是**企业级应用开发与运维**。在上海数药智能科技任职期间，我主要负责睡眠监测App的后端架构搭建。我使用Django、MySQL和Redis构建了高可用的服务，并通过Nginx和uWSGI进行部署。这段经历让我对高并发下的数据缓存策略和服务器性能优化有了深入的理解。同时，我也熟练使用Vue.js进行前端开发，能够独立完成前后端分离的项目交付。

第二是**桌面应用开发与自动化效能提升**。在最近的开立空调项目中，我负责研发内部的仿真工具。接手时该项目为前后端不分离的单体架构，界面卡顿、交互体验差。我复用上一个项目的前后端分离设计思路，利用PyQt5的QThread+信号槽机制将界面与仿真计算逻辑解耦，解决了长时计算阻塞主线程导致UI冻结的问题。同时，我搭建了Jenkins自动化流水线，覆盖代码检查、单元测试、回归测试、打包发布全流程，将交付效率从手动半天提升到自动20分钟。详见 [[05-个人/面试/开立空调仿真工具 项目面试 QA]]。

### 【数据与算法能力】

除了工程开发，我还具备一定的数据分析能力。我曾使用Pandas和Sklearn处理过脑波数据并训练分类模型，这让我不仅能写代码，也能理解业务背后的数据逻辑。

### 【结语】

总的来说，我是一个注重代码质量和业务落地的开发者。我对上海的工作机会非常感兴趣，也相信我的全栈开发能力和自动化测试经验能胜任贵公司的岗位。谢谢！

### 1. Opening · 开场

> Hello, my name is Liu Fangxuan. I have been working as a backend developer for four years, and my main language is Python. I write server-side code, build APIs, and also do some front-end work. I am comfortable with the whole process — from understanding what users need, to writing the code, to putting it online.
>
> **中文**：面试官您好，我叫刘方轩。我做了四年后端开发，主要用 Python。我写服务端代码和接口，也做一些前端。从需求到上线，我可以独立完成整个流程。

---

### 2. First Job — Web Backend · 第一份工作

> My first job was at a tech company in Shanghai. I built the backend for a sleep-tracking app. It was a mobile app — users would wear a device at night, and the app would collect their sleep data. I used Django and MySQL to store the data, and Redis to make the system faster. I also wrote some front-end pages with Vue.js. This job taught me how to build a real product that people actually use every day, and how to keep it stable when many users are online at the same time.
>
> **中文**：我第一份工作在上海一家科技公司，负责一个睡眠监测 App 的后台。用户晚上戴着设备睡觉，App 会收集睡眠数据。我用 Django 和 MySQL 存数据，用 Redis 做缓存提升速度，还用 Vue.js 写了一些前端页面。这段经历让我学会了怎么做一个真正有人用的产品，以及人多的时候系统怎么稳住。

---

### 3. Second Job — Desktop Tool & Automation · 第二份工作

> My second job was at an air-conditioning company. I built a simulation tool for the engineers there. When I joined, the old tool had a big problem: when it was running a heavy task, the whole window would freeze and you couldn't click anything. The reason was simple — the UI and the heavy math were running on the same thread, so one blocked the other.
>
> **中文**：我第二份工作在开立空调，给工程师们做了一个仿真工具。我接手的时候，旧工具有个大问题：一跑重任务，整个界面就卡住，点什么都没反应。原因很简单——界面和计算跑在同一个线程上，互相堵住了。
>
> I fixed this by moving the heavy work to a background thread. The UI stays smooth no matter how hard the math runs. I also set up an automatic testing and release pipeline with Jenkins. Before, it took four hours to test and release a new version by hand. After, it takes just 20 minutes, and the computer does almost all of it.
>
> **中文**：我把重计算搬到后台线程，界面不管怎么算都不卡了。然后我用 Jenkins 搭了一套自动测试和发布的流水线。以前手动测一次、发一个版本要半天，现在 20 分钟就搞定，基本不用人管。

---

### 4. A Little Extra · 一点补充

> Besides backend work, I have also done some data analysis. I used Python to work with brainwave data and train simple machine learning models. It's not my main skill, but it helps me understand the data side of a project, not just the code.
>
> **中文**：除了后端开发，我也做过一点数据分析。用 Python 处理过脑电波数据，训练过简单的机器学习模型。这不是我的主业，但它让我在看一个项目的时候能理解数据层面，而不只会看代码。

---

### 5. Closing · 收尾

> So, that's me in a nutshell — a Python developer with four years of experience, who can build a system from scratch, fix a broken one, and automate the boring stuff. I am really interested in this role, and I would love to bring my skills to your team. Thank you for your time.
>
> **中文**：总结一下：四年 Python 经验，能从头搭系统，也能接手修老项目，还能把重复劳动自动化。我对这个岗位非常感兴趣，希望有机会加入贵公司。谢谢。
>
> *(~450 words · ~3.5 minutes at normal speed)*

## 🎤 外企面试常见英文问题 & 回答思路

### 1. Tell me about yourself.
**（请简单介绍一下你自己。）**

> **回答思路**：控制在 1–2 分钟。用"现在 → 过去 → 未来"三段式：现在做什么（当前岗位/技术栈）→ 过去的关键成就（1–2 个亮点项目）→ 为什么对这个岗位感兴趣。
>
> **Model Answer**:
> "I'm a backend developer with four years of experience, primarily in Python. Currently, I focus on building scalable backend services and automation tooling. In my previous role, I redesigned a simulation tool's architecture, which cut the delivery cycle from half a day to 20 minutes through CI/CD automation. I'm now looking for a role where I can apply my full-stack and automation skills at a larger scale — and your company's work in [领域] really excites me."

---

### 2. Why do you want to work here? / Why this company?
**（你为什么想来我们公司工作？）**

> **回答思路**：不要只说"公司很好"。提前研究公司，从三个角度切入：技术栈匹配（你能贡献什么）→ 业务方向认同（你为什么在意）→ 成长空间（你能学到什么）。具体化是关键。
>
> **Model Answer**:
> "I've been following your company's work in [领域/产品] for a while, and I'm impressed by [具体产品/技术]. From a technical standpoint, your stack aligns well with my experience in Python, distributed systems, and automation — I can contribute from day one. Beyond that, I believe working here would push me to grow in [具体方向], which is exactly where I want to take my career."

---

### 3. What is your biggest weakness?
**（你最大的缺点是什么？）**

> **回答思路**：诚实但不自毁。公式：真实弱点 + 你已经采取的具体改进措施 + 改进效果。避免"我太努力了"这类虚伪答案。
>
> **Model Answer**:
> "I tend to dive too deep into technical details early on, which sometimes slows me down before the requirements are fully clear. I've been working on this by consciously separating exploration from execution — I now time-box my research phase and check in with stakeholders earlier. It's made a noticeable difference in my delivery speed without sacrificing quality."

---

### 4. Tell me about a challenging project and how you handled it.
**（讲一个你遇到的有挑战性的项目，以及你是如何处理的。）**

> **回答思路**：用 **STAR 法则**（Situation → Task → Action → Result）。选你最熟悉的一个项目（比如开立空调仿真工具），提前准备好量化数据。
>
> **Model Answer**:
> "In my last role, I inherited a simulation tool that had a critical problem — the UI would freeze completely during long computations because everything ran on the main thread. (Situation) My task was to fix this without a full rewrite. (Task) I analyzed the architecture and realized the root cause was tight coupling between the UI layer and the computation engine. I refactored it using PyQt5's QThread and signal-slot mechanism to decouple them, and set up a Jenkins pipeline for automated testing and deployment. (Action) The result: zero UI freezes, and the release cycle dropped from 4 hours to 20 minutes. (Result)"

---

### 5. Where do you see yourself in 3–5 years?
**（你未来 3 到 5 年的职业规划是什么？）**

> **回答思路**：展示你有方向但不僵化。从技术深度 + 影响力两个维度来谈。不要说你打算跳槽或转行。
>
> **Model Answer**:
> "In the near term, I want to deepen my expertise in distributed systems and backend architecture — ideally by working on systems at scale. In 3–5 years, I see myself growing into a senior engineer or tech lead role where I can not only build reliable systems but also mentor junior engineers and drive technical decisions. I'm particularly interested in [公司技术方向], and I'd love to grow in that direction here."

---

### 6. How do you handle disagreements with teammates?
**（你如何处理与团队成员的意见不合？）**

> **回答思路**：展示你注重数据、尊重他人、结果导向。用具体例子更好。
>
> **Model Answer**:
> "I try to keep disagreements about the work, not the person. When I disagree, I first make sure I fully understand the other person's perspective — usually by asking questions rather than arguing. Then I bring data or concrete examples to the discussion. For instance, in a past project, a teammate and I disagreed on whether to use a monolithic or microservice approach. Instead of going back and forth, we each built a small prototype and compared the trade-offs. The data made the decision clear, and we ended up with a hybrid approach that took the best of both."

---

### 7. Why did you leave (or want to leave) your current job?
**（你为什么离开上一份工作？）**

> **回答思路**：向前看，不要抱怨前公司/前老板。公式：在上一份工作中学到了什么 + 为什么现在想寻求新的挑战。
>
> **Model Answer**:
> "I learned a lot in my previous roles — especially around building end-to-end products and automation. But I've reached a point where I want to work on systems at a larger scale, with a stronger engineering culture around me. Your team's focus on [具体方向] is exactly the kind of environment I'm looking for to take my skills to the next level."

---

### 8. Describe a time you failed. What did you learn?
**（讲一次你失败的经历，你从中学到了什么？）**

> **回答思路**：失败不可怕，不说"我没失败过"。重点在"learn"——你如何复盘、如何改进、之后如何避免重复犯错。
>
> **Model Answer**:
> "Early in my career, I once deployed a feature without adequate monitoring in place. A few hours later, a database connection leak caused a partial outage that I didn't notice until a user reported it. That stung, but it taught me two things: never deploy without observability, and always set up alerting before things go wrong. Since then, I've made monitoring and alerting a non-negotiable part of my deployment checklist, and I've never had a repeat incident."

---

### 9. Do you have any questions for us?
**（你有什么问题要问我们吗？）**

> **回答思路**：**一定要问！** 没问题 = 没兴趣。问能展示你思考深度的问题。
>
> **推荐问题清单**：
> • "What does success look like for this role in the first 6 months?" — 展示你结果导向
> • "How does the team handle technical debt alongside feature work?" — 展示你关心工程质量
> • "What's the team's engineering culture like — code review norms, testing expectations, deployment cadence?" — 展示你在意协作方式
> • "What's the biggest technical challenge the team is facing right now?" — 展示你想解决问题而非只是拿 offer

---

### 10. Walk me through your resume.
**（请过一遍你的简历。）**

> **回答思路**：不要逐行念简历！按时间线挑关键点讲，每个职位用一句话概括职责 + 一个亮点数据。
>
> **Model Answer**:
> "I started my career at Shanghai Shùyào, where I built and deployed the backend for a sleep-monitoring app — that's where I got my foundation in Django, MySQL, Redis, and handling production traffic. I also picked up Vue.js there, so I've been comfortable full-stack from early on. Then I moved to Kāilì, where I took on a more challenging role — refactoring a legacy simulation tool with PyQt5 and setting up a CI/CD pipeline that cut delivery time from 4 hours to 20 minutes. Along the way, I've also done data analysis work with Pandas and Scikit-learn on EEG data. My strongest skills are in Python backend development, automation, and owning the full delivery lifecycle."

## 🧠 答题通用法则

| 法则 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **STAR** | Situation → Task → Action → Result，所有行为类问题都用这个框架 |
| **向前看** | 谈离职原因、谈失败经历，最终落脚点永远是"我学到了什么"和"我变成了更好的工程师" |
| **具体化** | 用数据说话（20 分钟 vs 半天、QPS、响应时间），避免模糊形容 |
| **真实** | 外企面试官更容易识破套话。不知道就说不知道，但补充"我会这样去搞清楚" |
| **反问** | 至少准备 2–3 个反问问题，展示你的主动性和思考深度 |
